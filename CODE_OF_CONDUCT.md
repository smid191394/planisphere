# Code of conduct

Be civil, and argue about the work rather than about the person doing it.

Issues and pull requests are about a drawing, an analyzer or a line of code.
Say what is wrong, what you expected, and what you would do instead. Disagree
as plainly as you like; insults, harassment and dismissal of someone for who
they are have no place here, and anything of that kind will be removed.

If something needs attention, say so on the issue or, where it should not be
public, in a [security advisory](https://github.com/smid191394/planisphere/security/advisories/new).
